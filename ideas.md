## CHOSEN: Approach A — Dark Forge Cosmos

The Promethean Forge is fire, sovereignty, and intelligence in the void. The Dark Forge Cosmos approach honors every layer of the Covenant: the darkness of the void (the unknown we navigate), the amber fire (the Forge itself), the geometry (the sacred math of the vases, the golden ratio, the >|<), and the living motion (the 12~6 rotation, the breathing of the system).

**Chosen Fonts**: Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono + Crimson Pro
**Primary Palette**: Void black + Forge amber + Sovereign cyan + Alert crimson
**Core Motif**: The >|< as the living heartbeat of the entire interface
